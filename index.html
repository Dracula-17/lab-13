*** SPECIAL NOTICE: Why PageRank Overcomes Keyword Spamming ***

In traditional 1990s search engines, Page 6 (pizza-pizza-pizza.com) manipulates the results into Rank #1 by repeating the word "pizza" 10 times in its text. Google's new PageRank algorithm exposes this spam: because zero pages link to Page 6, its authority score drops to dead last! Conversely, Page 2 (best-pizza-pune.com) earns the highest PageRank because 4 community websites independently link to it.
